WordBlush Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

WordBlush ("the app") is an Android word-search game published by Bharathi Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress, coins, stars, settings, daily-reward streak, collected words and the player name you choose. This data stays on your device. We do not create accounts and we do not upload this data to our servers. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is removed.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and general device and app information to serve and measure ads, and to show personalised ads where permitted. See Google's advertising policy and Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Analytics and crash reports

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the game is used (for example, which levels are started and finished) and to receive crash reports so we can fix bugs. Firebase may collect an app instance ID, device model, operating system version, approximate country, and crash logs. It does not collect your name or email address. See Firebase privacy information.

In-app purchases

You can buy coin packs through Google Play. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details; we only receive confirmation that a purchase completed.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos or files. The player name you enter is shown only inside the app on your device.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the service providers described above (Google AdMob, Google Firebase and Google Play) as needed to run ads and purchases.

Your choices

You can delete all locally stored game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it, and you can control ad personalisation in your device settings.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data (for example by adding new features or services), we will update this page and the date above.
